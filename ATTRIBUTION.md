# Data attribution

The word data shipped in `public/data/` is generated by `scripts/data/build.ts` (`pnpm data`)
from the sources below. The generated files are a derivative of Wiktionary and are therefore
distributed under **CC BY-SA 4.0**.

| Source | Used for | License |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Wiktionary](https://www.wiktionary.org/), English and Italian editions, extracted by [Wiktextract](https://github.com/tatuylonen/wiktextract) and published on [kaikki.org](https://kaikki.org/) | translations, definitions, inflected forms | [CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/) |
| [Tatoeba](https://tatoeba.org/) | example sentences and their translations | [CC BY 2.0 FR](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/2.0/fr/) |
| [FrequencyWords](https://github.com/hermitdave/FrequencyWords) by Hermit Dave (OpenSubtitles 2018) | word frequency ranking | MIT |

Rebuilding needs about 3.5 GB of downloads, which are cached in `.data-cache/`.
